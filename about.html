About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery opened on Harbour Lane in 2007. Our founder, Morag Campbell, grew up baking with her gran, and a lot of what we sell still comes from those family recipes, like our Signature Loaf and shortbread. It started as a small shop with just Morag and one helper. Now we have a team of six, but we still bake everything here in St Amelia.

Where Our Ingredients Come From

We try to buy as close to home as we can.

  • Our flour comes from a mill here in Scotland
  • Butter, milk, and eggs come from farms in Fife
  • We use fruit that's in season, so some items change through the year
  • Leftover bread goes to the local food bank at the end of the day
